# ⚠️ Aztec Validator Shutdown Notice — Action Required from Delegators

Hi everyone,

I actually made the decision to wind down my Aztec validator quite some time ago, mainly due to the current economics of running a validator and, to some extent, uncertainty about the project's future direction.

I've already asked my delegators in our group to undelegate as soon as possible, but unfortunately, it seems those messages haven't reached everyone. So I've decided to post here as well.

## Why I Can't Simply Shut Down

The main issue is that **there's currently no mechanism that allows me, as a provider, to voluntarily exit the network or unilaterally remove your delegations.**

If I simply shut down my servers while **my sequencers, which have your stake delegated to them, remain in the active set, you — my delegators — would be the ones facing slashing penalties and potentially losing your funds.**

At the same time, I have no way to undelegate on your behalf or protect your stake once my infrastructure goes offline. Only you can initiate undelegation.

This issue has been raised multiple times, including on Discord and the forum, but unfortunately, there's still no solution.

So I'm stuck in a situation where I can't safely shut down my infrastructure, but I also can't seem to reach my delegators.

## Temporary Operator Fee Increase to 92.5%

**As a last resort, I've decided to temporarily increase my operator fee to 92.5%.**

I want to make it absolutely clear that **this is not an attempt to extract additional rewards from you.**

The idea is simply to make the significant reduction in your rewards noticeable enough that you'll check what's going on, find this announcement, and hopefully undelegate.

I'll continue keeping my sequencers online during this transition to avoid exposing your stake to unnecessary slashing risk.

I genuinely don't see another practical way to get everyone's attention without putting your funds at risk.

## ⚠️ Action Required: Please Undelegate

**If you've delegated to my validator, please undelegate and move your stake to another provider as soon as possible.**

I appreciate everyone who has supported my validator, and I'm sorry it has to end this way.

I feel responsible for your funds and don't want anyone to suffer losses simply because I've decided to stop operating.

**My priority is to make sure all of you can exit safely.**
